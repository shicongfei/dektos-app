original: |
  道可道，非常道；名可名，非常名。
  无名天地之始；有名万物之母。
  故常无欲，以观其妙；常有欲，以观其徼。
  此两者，同出而异名，同谓之玄。
  玄之又玄，众妙之门。

note_en: The Chinese Union Version renders the Greek Logos as Dao. Laozi says the true Dao cannot be fully spoken; John says this Word was there in the beginning. Both texts open with a source prior to all things that no name can exhaust.
